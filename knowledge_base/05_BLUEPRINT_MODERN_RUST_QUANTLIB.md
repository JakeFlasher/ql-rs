# ql-rs Software Architecture and Mathematical Blueprint

## 1. Core tenets

ql-rs uses immutable market snapshots and resolved contracts, generic `Real`/`SimdReal` kernels, IFT at every root/calibration boundary, SoA and arena storage for hot loops, and a content-addressed dependency graph for incremental risk. Pricing functions are explicit `(contract, market, model) -> result` computations; no observer graph or global evaluation date is required.

## 2. Data flow

```text
Date / Calendar / Convention
  -> TradeSpec::resolve -> Resolved SoA cashflows
  -> MarketSnapshot -> calibrated curves and surfaces (IFT Jacobians)
  -> model, PDE, COS, MC, or VM engine (f64 / SIMD / AD)
  -> PortfolioDag / NpvCube / SIMM / terminal output
```

## 3. Crate walkthrough

### 3.1 Foundations: `ql-time`, `ql-ad`, `ql-math`

`ql-time` stores `Date(i32)` serials, civil-date conversions, exchange calendars, day-count conventions, IMM rules, and schedule generation. `BitsetCalendar` packs business-day masks and counts ranges with POPCNT; schedule resolution produces explicit accrual and payment dates.

`ql-ad` defines `Real` and `SimdReal`, so formulas are generic over `f64`, SIMD lanes, `Dual<N>`, `Dual2<N>`, and reverse `TapeVar`. `Dual` carries a fixed gradient, `Dual2` carries a gradient and dense Hessian, and `ArenaTape` stores value/derivative/parent arrays in a rewindable index arena. `ift_lift_1d` and related helpers lift primal solver roots through local Jacobians without recording iterations.

`ql-math` supplies monotone and natural cubic interpolation, univariate and bivariate normal distributions (`bivariate_normal_cdf` via Genz/West/Drezner), elliptical and Archimedean copulas (`GaussianCopula`, `ClaytonCopula`, `FrankCopula`, `GumbelCopula`, `FarlieGumbelMorgensternCopula`), Brent/Newton roots, generic tridiagonal/Thomas solves, Gaussian rules, and Levenberg–Marquardt. These are deliberately scalar-generic and form the numerical substrate for every higher layer.

### 3.2 Curves, volatility, contracts, and rates

`ql-curves` builds OIS discount and IBOR forwarding curves from deposits, FRAs, IRS, and OIS helpers. At a converged pillar vector `theta`, quote sensitivities use Henrard's IFT relation $d\theta/dq=-J_\theta^{-1}J_q$; cached LU factors make repeated VJPs cheap. `ql-vol` wraps Black/Bachelier inversion, SVI/eSSVI/SABR surfaces with Dual/Dual2 sensitivities, Gatheral–Jacquier (2014) Durrleman butterfly-arbitrage density checks (`svi_durrleman_g`), and `CalibratedSabrSmile` / `CalibratedSviSmile` with second-order optimality IFT (`Dual2<N>`) quote sensitivities.

`ql-instruments` resolves swaps, bonds, options, and coupon legs into contiguous arrays (`ResolvedCouponLeg`) for scalar and SIMD valuation, and provides analytical/free-boundary American option approximations (`BaroneAdesiWhaleyEngine`, `BjerksundStensland1993Engine`, `BjerksundStensland2002Engine`, `JuQuadraticEngine`) with `ift_lift_1d` critical boundary and `american_implied_vol` inversion. `ql-fixed-income` adds Nelson–Siegel–Svensson zero/forward curves, Black yield-to-price callable bonds, event-aware short-rate trees, OAS duration/convexity, Tsiveriotis–Fernandes convertible-bond induction with cash floors, De Spiegeleer–Schoutens (2011) Basel III AT1 Contingent Convertible (`CoCo`) bonds (`CocoBondSpec`, `price_coco_bond_ds`), amortizing sinking-fund bonds, CPI inflation-linked bonds, PSA/CPR and Richard–Roll refinancing S-curve + default/severity (`SpecifiedPoolMbsSpec`, `price_specified_pool_mbs`) agency/credit MBS pass-through cashflow and OAS engines, TBA Dollar Roll & Implied Repo analytics (`TbaDollarRollSpec`, `analyze_tba_dollar_roll`), Agency CMO Sequential / PAC / Support / IO-PO Waterfall engines (`CmoWaterfallSpec`, `price_cmo_waterfall`), Treasury Bond Futures Conversion Factor, Implied Repo, Cheapest-to-Deliver (`CTD`), and Switch Option analytics (`TreasuryFuturesBasketSpec`, `analyze_treasury_futures_ctd`), Par-Par & Market-Value Asset Swap Spreads (`ASW`) and IFT `Z-Spread` (`BondAssetSwapSpec`, `analyze_bond_asset_swap_and_z_spread`), and insurance-linked Catastrophe (`CAT`) bonds (`DigitalNotionalRisk` and `ProportionalNotionalRisk` under Poisson–Exponential, Poisson–Pareto, and compound-Poisson loss processes). `ql-rates` adds Ibor caplet stripping, path-dependent Sticky and Ratchet cap/floors (`StickyRatchetCapFloorSpec`, `price_sticky_ratchet_capfloor_hw1f`), smile-replicated IBOR/CMS Range Accrual coupons (`RangeAccrualSpec`, `price_range_accrual_coupon`), physical/cash European, Mid-Curve (`MidCurveSwaptionSpec`, `price_midcurve_swaption_hw1f`, `price_midcurve_swaption_g2pp`), and Bermudan swaptions (`BermudanSwaption`, `HullWhiteBermudanTreeEngine`, `Fd1dHullWhiteBermudanEngine`), 3D Swaption SABR Volatility Cubes (`SwaptionSabrCube`, `CalibratedSwaptionSabrCube`), SABR Bachelier pricing, G2++, Cheyette local variance and Andersen–Piterbarg (2010) Markovian projection swaption pricing (`CheyetteSwaptionSpec`, `price_cheyette_swaption_markov_projection`), Hunt–Kennedy–Pelsser (2000) 1F Markov-Functional Terminal Swap Rate swaption/digital/CMS pricing (`MarkovFunctionalSwaptionSpec`, `price_markov_functional_1f`), Linear-TSR CMS convexity, Brigo–Mercurio / Caspers `LognormalCmsSpreadPricer` for CMS spread coupons/caplets/floorlets/collars, and post-LIBOR backward-looking RFR (SOFR/SONIA/€STR) caplets/floorlets, Willems (2020) effective SABR smiles, Hull–White RFR caplets, and 1M/3M SOFR futures.

### 3.3 Stochastic models and numerical pricing

`ql-models` implements Heston/Bates and Christoffersen–Heston–Jacobs (2009) Two-Factor Double Heston (`DoubleHestonParams`, `double_heston_cos_price`, `double_heston_cos_strip_prices`) characteristic functions, COS prices, Lin–Heston–Bates VIX futures and options, Bergomi (2005) Two-Factor forward variance curve & Bergomi–Guyon (2012) order-2 smile expansion (`Bergomi2FParams`, `bergomi_2f_implied_vol_bg`, `bergomi_2f_mc_price`), Gatheral–Jusselin–Rosenbaum (2020) Quadratic Rough Heston (`QuadraticRoughHestonParams`, `qrh_implied_vol_expansion`, `qrh_mc_price`), Hull–White Jamshidian, exact `Dual2<2>` Dupire local volatility, Andreasen–Huge (2011) arbitrage-free one-step finite-difference local volatility interpolation (`AndreasenHugeGridSpec`, `solve_andreasen_huge_step`, `interpolate_andreasen_huge_call`), an Abi Jaber Markovian rough-Heston lift and Quintic OU joint SPX/VIX engine, and Equity/FX–Rates hybrid engines (`AnalyticBsmHullWhiteEngine` and Grzelak–Oosterlee 2011 `H1-HW` `CosHestonHullWhiteEngine`). `ql-levy` implements Merton, Kou, Variance-Gamma, and CGMY characteristic exponents, exact Merton Poisson series, Fang–Oosterlee COS pricing with Junike–Pankrashkin cumulant truncation, Lewis (2001) strip contour integration (`lewis_levy_price_strip`), and Carr–Madan (1999) damped Radix-2 FFT option strip pricing (`carr_madan_fft_levy_price_strip`).

`ql-pde` solves Heston PDEs with sinh grids, mixed derivatives, boundary conditions, Rannacher damping, and Douglas/Craig–Sneyd/Modified Craig–Sneyd/Hundsdorfer–Verwer ADI sweeps with full AD time-step sensitivity. `ql-mc` provides stateless Philox, Sobol and Brownian bridges, SoA SIMD GBM, Andersen QE Heston, checkpointed pathwise AAD, two-stage Longstaff–Schwartz AMC with Glasserman envelope-theorem pathwise AD (`price_with_policy_ad`), and Andersen–Broadie (2004) primal-dual Bermudan upper-bound Monte Carlo. Burley hash-based Owen scrambling and bridge antithetics provide randomized QMC with standard errors.

### 3.5 Scripting, XVA, learning, and portfolios

`ql-scripting` lowers declarative products to flat opcodes and evaluates them generically with Fuzzy AAD smoothing for triggers. `ql-xva` simulates `NpvCube`, applies CSA collateral, computes CVA/DVA/FVA, Burgard–Kjaer asymmetric funding & collateral XVA (`FCA`, `FBA`, `ColVA` via `compute_bilateral_xva`), Gaussian-copula correlated first-to-default bilateral XVA (`compute_correlated_first_to_default_bilateral_xva`), Hull–White (2012) / Brigo–Vrins (2018) hazard-rate exposure-coupled Wrong-Way Risk (`compute_xva_with_wwr`), Andersen–Pykhtin–Sokol (2017) MPoR XVA and Contingent CDS, Basel III SA-CCR regulatory Exposure-at-Default (`compute_sa_ccr_ead`), Basel III MAR50 Basic Approach CVA Capital (`BaCvaPortfolioSpec`, `compute_ba_cva_capital`), Green–Kenyon–Dennis (2014) Capital Valuation Adjustment (`compute_kva`), ISDA SIMM, FRTB SBM/IMA, and Chebyshev DIM/MVA profiles. `ql-dml` supplies classical and differential PCA, multi-index differential polynomial regression, Differential Neural Networks and PLS, and Capriotti–Giles adjoint Cholesky correlation Greeks.

`ql-portfolio` hashes market dependencies, caches calibrated curves and trade results, evaluates dirty trades in Rayon, maps Dual/IFT sensitivities to a portfolio summary, computes empirical and parametric coherent risk measures (Acerbi–Tasche Expected Shortfall / CVaR, Ahmadi-Javid 2012 Entropic Value-at-Risk `entropic_value_at_risk` & `parametric_gaussian_evar`, Chekhlov–Uryasev–Zabarankin 2005 Conditional Drawdown-at-Risk `conditional_drawdown_at_risk`, Mean-CDaR optimization, Euler Factor Risk Attribution `factor_risk_attribution`, Cornish–Fisher VaR/ES, Markowitz semi-deviation, regret, and Euler homogeneous AAD risk allocation), and provides institutional portfolio allocation and execution engines: He–Litterman (1999) Black–Litterman Bayesian posterior, Idzorek (2005) singularity-free confidence-weighted Black–Litterman (`IdzorekBlackLittermanSpec`, `idzorek_black_litterman_posterior`), Busseti–Ryu–Boyd (2016) drawdown-constrained fractional Kelly (`KellyAllocationSpec`, `kelly_fractional_allocation`), unconstrained, box-constrained, and Goldfarb–Iyengar (2003) robust Markowitz Mean–Variance & Tangency with active-set KKT implicit differentiation, Spinu / Bruder–Roncalli Risk Budgeting / Equal Risk Contribution with multivariate IFT Hessian sensitivity lifting, López de Prado (2016) Hierarchical Risk Parity (`try_hierarchical_risk_parity`, `hierarchical_risk_parity_weights`), Gerber–Markowitz (2022) robust co-movement covariance (`gerber_covariance_matrix`), Marchenko–Pastur RMT eigenvalue denoising (`marchenko_pastur_denoise_covariance`), López de Prado (2019) Nested Clustered Optimization (`NestedClusteredOptimizationSpec`, `nested_clustered_optimization_weights`), single- and multi-asset Almgren–Chriss (`try_multi_asset_almgren_chriss_execution`), Obizhaeva–Wang, and Kratz–Schöneborn (2014) continuous-time Dark-Pool + Lit-Venue optimal execution (`DarkLitRoutingSpec`, `optimal_dark_lit_execution`). Together these crates turn individual pricing kernels into scalable scenario, counterparty, and asset-allocation risk.

### 3.6 Delivery, measurements, and workstation

`ql-benches` is an offline statistical harness with fixed workloads, warmups, medians, IQRs, speedups, and hard parity assertions across 50 suites (`01_calendar_popcnt` through `50_hagan_west_tension_duffie_lando_pac_cmo_fama_macbeth_shanken_gatheral_asvi_bgk_discrete_barrier_bouchaud_cross_impact_karnosky_singer_merton_jump_campbell_viceira_ohlson_lid_griewank_revolve`). `ql-terminal` re-exports `ql_terminal::prelude::*` and renders a zero-dependency 48-tab risk workstation: a $2 \times 4$ sub-pixel `BrailleCanvas`, 24-bit ANSI TrueColor ladders, live blotter values, XVA/SIMM/MVA/SA-CCR/BA-CVA/KVA/FRTB, and calibrated chart series.
